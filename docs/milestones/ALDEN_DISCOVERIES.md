# Alden discovers ... (the milestone pictures)

At every major NoorEngine milestone, Alden Wayfarer discovers what the engine just learned (the tradition from
devlog #015, Noor's idea). Each review has one line, and each line its picture: **all ten painted by Noor on 2026-11-20**, the same day
they were asked for. This page lists them all, oldest first.

**Alden, as the concept sheet has him** (please keep to it): a blocky low-poly explorer; short brown hair and a beard;
a teal/green tunic with rolled sleeves; a brown belt with a gold square buckle; dark brown trousers and boots; a big
brown backpack with a rolled bedroll on top; a wooden walking staff. Palette: tan skin, browns, teal. Reference:
`docs/devlog/img/devlog015_alden_concept.webp`.

Milestones 1 to 3, 7 and 8A had no line in their reviews until the pictures came; the lines Noor painted were added
then. Milestones 1 to 3 came before the tradition began.

| 2 | M2: projects, assets and scenes | Saving real content reliably: projects, assets that keep their identity, scenes | **... that the world remembers.** He sets a stone on a cairn, walks away, comes back the next day, and it's still there, exactly where he left it. | in the review | [Noor's](../devlog/img/alden/alden_m2_memory.webp) |
